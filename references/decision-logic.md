# Decision Logic

This file expands the decision logic referenced by `SKILL.md`.

## 1. Planning window

For each refresh, use:

**request date → next 10 days**

Do not plan from a vague month label alone.

## 2. Research order

Check, when relevant:

1. UK weather / seasonal transition
2. nearby UK retail or holiday moments
3. current competitor promotion structures
4. LampsModern category/sales context
5. immediately previous homepage banner history
6. scene suitability and visual freshness

## 3. Weather is an input, not the whole answer

Weather can change which product category or scene is more relevant.

Examples:
- wetter/darker period → indoor comfort or entrance/outdoor safety may become more relevant
- warmer/brighter period → outdoor/garden or ceiling-fan themes may become more relevant

Do not turn a weather signal directly into “clearance” unless there is a real clearance reason.

## 4. Category choice

Record category at three levels:

`Broad Category / Subcategory or Style / Scene`

Examples:
- Ceiling Lights / Crystal Ceiling Lights / Living Room
- Pendant Lights / Dining Pendants / Dining Room
- Outdoor Lighting / Outdoor Wall Lights / Front Entrance

## 5. Repeat handling

Only the immediately previous banner pair is the default repeat check.

A repeat is allowed when justified by:
- stronger sales/category importance
- seasonal fit
- weather relevance
- event fit
- better conversion logic

If repeated, explicitly explain why.

## 6. Discount strategy

Discounts are used to support conversion and basket value.

Prefer threshold/tiered mechanics when suitable.

The theme itself should not be “increase AOV”.

Current competitor offers may be researched as context, but:
- do not copy blindly
- do not invent current offers
- do not treat competitor thresholds as mandatory

## 7. Default two-banner relationship

The two banners should feel related to the same period, but should not communicate the same emotional idea twice.

Good relationship:
- Primary: indoor comfort + promotion
- Secondary: outdoor arrival/weather story

Avoid:
- two banners both saying “cosy evenings”
- two banners both using the same category, scene and emotional language without a strong reason
